# ISTQB Chapter 5 Glossary

## Key terms
- Test plan: A document describing the test approach, scope, and resources.
- Entry criteria: Conditions that must be met before testing begins.
- Exit criteria: Conditions that must be met before testing can be considered complete.
- Estimation: Predicting the effort required for testing.
- Test case prioritization: Deciding which tests should be executed first.
- Test pyramid: A model showing the balance between unit, integration, and UI tests.
- Testing quadrants: A model that classifies testing by purpose and level.
- Risk: The possibility of a negative event or outcome.
- Project risk: A risk related to the project or organization.
- Product risk: A risk related to the quality or behavior of the product.
- Risk analysis: Evaluating likelihood and impact.
- Risk control: Actions taken to reduce or manage risk.
- Configuration management: Controlling changes to work products and their versions.
- Defect management: The process of reporting, tracking, and resolving defects.
- Defect report: A document describing a defect and its details.
