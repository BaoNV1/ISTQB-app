# ISTQB CTFL v4.0.1 – Mock Exam (40 Questions)
## Answer Key + Detailed Explanations

---

**1. B**  
Finding defects is one of the main objectives of testing. Fixing defects is part of debugging.

**2. B**  
The correct sequence is: Error (human mistake) → Defect (in the work product) → Failure (when the system is executed).

**3. B**  
The first testing principle states that testing can show the presence of defects but cannot prove their absence.

**4. A**  
Testing discovers defects/failures. Debugging finds the root cause and fixes the defect.

**5. B**  
Whole Team Approach means everyone in the team shares responsibility for quality.

**6. B**  
Test Design is one of the main activities in the test process.

**7. B**  
Shift-Left means performing testing activities earlier in the software development lifecycle.

**8. C**  
Component (Unit) Testing is typically performed by developers.

**9. B**  
Confirmation Testing is also called Retesting (checking that a specific defect has been fixed).

**10. B**  
Maintenance Testing is triggered by modifications, migration, or retirement of the system.

**11. B**  
Continuous Integration (CI) provides fast feedback on code quality in DevOps.

**12. B**  
Static Testing examines work products without executing the software.

**13. D**  
Inspection is the most formal type of review.

**14. B**  
In a Walkthrough, the Author leads the session and explains the work product.

**15. B**  
A major benefit of static testing is early defect detection, which reduces the cost of fixing defects.

**16. C**  
The Scribe (Recorder) documents issues, decisions, and action items during the review.

**17. B**  
Equivalence Partitioning divides data into groups (partitions) that are expected to be processed the same way.

**18. B**  
Partitions: age < 18 (invalid), 18–60 (valid), age > 60 (invalid) → 3 partitions.

**19. B**  
Decision Table Testing is the most suitable technique for combinations of business conditions and actions.

**20. A**  
100% Branch Coverage always guarantees 100% Statement Coverage (but not the other way around).

**21. B**  
In ATDD, acceptance tests are created collaboratively by the team before implementation.

**22. B**  
Covering all valid transitions is stronger than covering only the states.

**23. B**  
Error Guessing relies on the tester’s experience and knowledge to anticipate likely defects.

**24. B**  
A Test Plan describes the objectives, approach, resources, schedule, and other important aspects of testing.

**25. B**  
Risk Level = Likelihood × Impact.

**26. C**  
Product Risks are related to the quality of the product (e.g., security vulnerabilities, crashes, functional failures).

**27. B**  
Entry Criteria define the conditions that must be satisfied before testing can begin.

**28. B**  
The Test Pyramid recommends many automated unit tests at the bottom and fewer high-level end-to-end tests at the top.

**29. B**  
A good defect report should include steps to reproduce, expected result, actual result, and severity/priority.

**30. B**  
Test Monitoring is the activity of collecting information about testing progress and comparing it against the plan.

**31. B**  
Metrics-based estimation uses historical data from previous projects.

**32. B**  
Configuration Management controls versions of work products (code, tests, documents) and supports traceability.

**33. C**  
Exit Criteria are the conditions that must be met before testing can be considered complete (e.g., critical defects fixed + coverage targets achieved).

**34. B**  
Testing Quadrants help classify different types of tests (business-facing vs technology-facing, supporting the team vs critiquing the product).

**35. B**  
Product risk analysis influences the scope, depth, and prioritization of testing.

**36. B**  
Achieving 100% Branch Coverage automatically means you have also achieved 100% Statement Coverage.

**37. B**  
In a Decision Table, each column usually represents one unique combination of conditions (one rule) and the related actions.

**38. B**  
Exploratory Testing is characterized by the simultaneous activities of learning, test design, and test execution.

**39. C**  
Project Risks are related to the project’s ability to deliver (people, schedule, budget, resources…).

**40. B**  
Risk-based Testing focuses more testing effort on the areas with higher risk.
