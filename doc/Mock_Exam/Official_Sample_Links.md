## Exam format (official)

- 40 questions  
- 60 minutes (75 minutes if English is not your primary language)  
- Pass mark: **26/40 (65%)**

## How to use with this learning app

1. Download the official PDFs from istqb.org.  
2. Practice them under timed conditions (60 minutes).  
3. Review the **justifications** in the Answers documents carefully.  
4. Use the **original practice quizzes** in this project (`doc/chapter*/` and `doc/Mock_Exam/`) for extra drills — those are study materials, **not** copies of the official samples.
