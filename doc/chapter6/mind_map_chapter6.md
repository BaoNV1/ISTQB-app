```mermaid
    6.2 Benefits and Risks of Test Automation
      Benefits
        Reduce repetitive manual work
        Greater consistency & repeatability
        Faster execution & earlier feedback
        More time for better test design
        Fewer human errors
      Risks
        Unrealistic expectations
        Underestimated cost & effort
        Using automation when manual is better
        High maintenance cost
        Over-reliance on automation
```

## Study focus
- Understand why tools are used in testing.
- Know the main benefits and risks of automation.
- Remember that tools support testing but do not replace thinking and judgment.
